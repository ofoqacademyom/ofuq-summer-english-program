# English Placement Test

This placement test is designed to measure the current English level of Grade 12 students before joining the Ofoq Summer English Program.

## Test Purpose

The purpose of this test is not to judge students, but to understand their current English level and identify their strengths and weaknesses.

The results will help the team:

- Group students according to their levels.
- Identify common grammar and vocabulary weaknesses.
- Design suitable lessons.
- Provide extra support for students who need it.
- Measure progress during the program.

## Test Sections

The test includes:

1. Grammar
2. Vocabulary
3. Reading Comprehension
4. Writing
5. Self-Assessment

---

## Section 1: Grammar

Choose the correct answer.

1. She ___ to school every day.  
   - go  
   - goes  
   - going  
   - gone  

2. They ___ watching a movie now.  
   - is  
   - are  
   - am  
   - be  

3. I have lived in Oman ___ 2010.  
   - since  
   - for  
   - at  
   - on  

4. If I study hard, I ___ pass the exam.  
   - will  
   - would  
   - can  
   - did  

5. The book ___ by many students.  
   - reads  
   - read  
   - is read  
   - reading  

6. Yesterday, I ___ my homework before dinner.  
   - finish  
   - finished  
   - finishing  
   - finishes  

7. This is the student ___ won the competition.  
   - who  
   - which  
   - where  
   - when  

8. You ___ wear a seatbelt in the car.  
   - must  
   - may  
   - might  
   - could

## Section 2: Vocabulary

1. What does "improve" mean?

2. What is the opposite of "difficult"?

3. Use the word "important" in a sentence.

4. Choose the correct meaning of "confident":
   - Afraid
   - Sure about yourself
   - Angry
   - Tired

5. Write two words related to school or learning.

6. What does "prepare" mean?

7. Use the word "goal" in a sentence.

---

## Section 3: Reading Comprehension

Read a short paragraph and answer questions about:

- Main idea
- Supporting details
- Vocabulary in context
- Author's purpose

Example questions:

1. What is the main idea of the paragraph?
2. Mention one detail from the paragraph.
3. What does the word "challenge" mean in the paragraph?
4. Why is English important for students?

---

## Section 4: Writing

Write a short paragraph about your goals for learning English.

Your paragraph should include:

- At least five sentences
- Clear ideas
- Correct punctuation
- Simple grammar
- One example of how English can help you in the future

---

## Section 5: Self-Assessment

Rate your English level:

- Beginner
- Pre-intermediate
- Intermediate
- Upper-intermediate
- Advanced

Answer the following questions:

1. What do you want to improve most in English?
2. What is your strongest English skill?
3. What is your weakest English skill?
4. How confident do you feel about learning English this summer?
