# Summer English Course Plan

This document outlines the proposed structure of the Ofoq Summer English Program for Grade 12 students in Oman.

## Course Overview

The program is designed as an online summer course that helps Grade 12 students improve their English skills before the academic year.

The course will focus on:

- Grammar foundations
- Vocabulary development
- Reading comprehension
- Writing practice
- Speaking confidence
- Exam preparation
- Study habits and self-learning skills

## Course Duration

Proposed duration: 4 weeks

Each week will include:

- Live online sessions
- Practice activities
- Short assignments
- Review materials
- Student support and feedback

## Week 1: English Foundations

Focus areas:

- Parts of speech
- Sentence structure
- Present simple and present continuous
- Common grammar mistakes
- Basic vocabulary for school and daily life

Learning outcome:

Students will review basic English structures and identify their common mistakes.

## Week 2: Grammar and Vocabulary Development

Focus areas:

- Past simple and past continuous
- Future forms
- Modal verbs
- Vocabulary building strategies
- Using words in sentences

Learning outcome:

Students will improve their ability to use grammar rules and vocabulary in simple contexts.

## Week 3: Reading and Writing Skills

Focus areas:

- Reading comprehension strategies
- Identifying main ideas
- Understanding details
- Paragraph writing
- Organizing ideas clearly

Learning outcome:

Students will improve their reading comprehension and write clearer short paragraphs.

## Week 4: Speaking, Review, and Exam Preparation

Focus areas:

- Speaking confidence
- Answering common questions
- Review of grammar and vocabulary
- Practice quizzes
- Final progress reflection

Learning outcome:

Students will review key skills, practice communication, and prepare for future English learning.

## Teaching Method

The program will use:

- Simple explanations
- Examples from student life
- Short practice activities
- Interactive questions
- Quizzes
- Group discussion
- AI-supported learning materials
- Student feedback

## Assessment

Students may be assessed through:

- Placement test before the course
- Short weekly quizzes
- Writing tasks
- Participation
- Final reflection
- Progress tracker

## Expected Outcomes

By the end of the program, students are expected to:

- Understand their English strengths and weaknesses
- Improve basic grammar knowledge
- Build useful vocabulary
- Write better short paragraphs
- Feel more confident in English
- Develop a clearer study plan for the academic year

## Future Development

This course plan will be improved based on:

- Student survey responses
- Placement test results
- Student feedback
- Teacher and volunteer observations
- Learning progress data
