# Pre-Launch Preparation Plan

This document explains the preparation phase before launching the Ofoq Summer English Program for Grade 12 students in Oman.

## 1. Define the Program Purpose

The program aims to:

- Support Grade 12 students in improving their English skills.
- Identify students' real learning needs before launching.
- Provide organized online learning resources.
- Help students prepare for the academic year.
- Build a free and reusable open educational resource.

## 2. Create a Student Needs Survey

Before designing the course, a student needs survey will be created.

The survey will help identify:

- Students' main difficulties in English.
- Weak areas in grammar, vocabulary, reading, writing, listening, and speaking.
- Students' preferred learning methods.
- Suitable times for online sessions.
- Students' expectations from the summer program.
- The type of resources students find most helpful.

## 3. Create an English Placement Test

A placement test will be created to measure students' current English level.

The test will include:

- Grammar questions.
- Vocabulary questions.
- Reading comprehension.
- A short writing task.
- A self-assessment section.

The goal is not to grade students harshly, but to understand their level and place them in suitable learning groups.

## 4. Analyze Student Data

After collecting survey and placement test responses, the team will analyze:

- Common weaknesses.
- Student levels.
- Most requested topics.
- Suitable class times.
- Preferred learning methods.
- Students who may need extra support.

## 5. Design the Summer Course Plan

Based on the results, the team will design:

- Weekly learning objectives.
- Lesson topics.
- Practice activities.
- Review sessions.
- Assignments.
- Student support system.
- Progress tracking method.

## 6. Prepare the Learning Platform

The program will use simple and accessible digital tools.

Possible tools include:

- Notion for course organization.
- Google Forms for surveys and tests.
- Google Sheets for progress tracking.
- WhatsApp or Telegram for communication.
- Google Meet or Zoom for live sessions.
- Canva for educational visuals.

## 7. Prepare AI-Supported Resources

AI tools may support:

- Creating practice questions.
- Simplifying grammar explanations.
- Generating vocabulary exercises.
- Reviewing lesson materials.
- Summarizing resources.
- Creating study guides.
- Supporting teachers and volunteers.

## 8. Prepare the Team

Before launching, the team should define clear roles.

Possible roles:

- Program coordinator.
- English content creator.
- Student support volunteer.
- Design and media volunteer.
- Data and survey coordinator.
- Session facilitator.

## 9. Launch Readiness Checklist

Before launching the program, the following items should be ready:

- Student needs survey.
- English placement test.
- Registration form.
- Course schedule.
- Online platform.
- Communication group.
- Learning materials.
- Teacher and volunteer roles.
- Student progress tracker.
- Announcement message.
- Feedback form.
