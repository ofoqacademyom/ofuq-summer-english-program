# Student Needs Survey

This survey is designed to understand the needs and challenges of Grade 12 students before launching the Ofoq Summer English Program.

## Survey Purpose

The purpose of this survey is to listen to students before designing the course. Instead of assuming what students need, the program will collect real responses and use them to build a more useful learning experience.

The results of this survey will help the team decide:

- Which English skills students struggle with most.
- What type of lessons students need.
- What learning methods students prefer.
- What time is suitable for online sessions.
- How the summer program should be organized.
